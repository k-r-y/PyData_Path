When reviewing regional sales orders, management often asks for the **runner-up unique** order value (strictly smaller than the highest value).

Write `second_largest_unique(nums: list[int | float]) -> int | float | None` that returns the second largest distinct number in `nums`. Return `None` if there are fewer than two distinct numbers.

### Constraints
- Values may be negative, zero, or contain repeated maxima.
- Do not modify the caller's list in place.
