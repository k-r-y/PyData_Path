Determine whether a partially filled `9 x 9` board satisfies all Sudoku constraints:
- Each filled digit (`'1'` through `'9'`) may appear at most once in any row, at most once in any column, and at most once inside each `3 x 3` sub-box.
- Empty cells are represented by `'.'`.

Write `is_valid_sudoku(board: list[list[str]]) -> bool`.
