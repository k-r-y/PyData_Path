Two background sync jobs repeat every `a` seconds and `b` seconds. Compute both their greatest common divisor and least common multiple without calling `math.gcd` or `math.lcm`.

Write `gcd_and_lcm(a: int, b: int) -> tuple[int, int]` returning non-negative `(gcd, lcm)`. When both are `0`, return `(0, 0)`.
