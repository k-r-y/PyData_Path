Evaluate an integer arithmetic expression in Reverse Polish Notation (postfix), returning the final integer value.

Write `eval_postfix(tokens: list[str]) -> int`:
- Operators are `"+"`, `"-"`, `"*"`, and `"/"`.
- Integer division truncates **toward zero** (for example `-7 / 2 -> -3`, NOT `-4`).
- Input is guaranteed non-empty and valid.
